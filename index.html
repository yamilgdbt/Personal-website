Boston Celtics

      

The Boston Celtics are a professional basketball team based in Boston, Massachusetts, and compete in the National Basketball Association (NBA) as a member of the Eastern Conference. Founded in 1946, the Celtics are one of the most historic and successful franchises in basketball, with a legacy built on championship success, legendary players, and a passionate fan base.

Throughout their history, the Celtics have featured some of the greatest players in NBA history, including [Bill Russel], Larry Bird, Paul Pierce, and Kevin Garnett. The team dominated the league during the 1950s and 1960s, winning eight consecutive NBA championships from 1959 to 1966. Later generations continued the franchise's tradition of excellence, with championship victories in 1981, 1984, 1986, 2008, and 2024


The Celtics play their home games at TD Garden in Boston and are recognized by their iconic green and white colors, shamrock logo, and longstanding rivalry with the Los Angeles Lakers. In 2024, Boston captured its 18th NBA championship, establishing the franchise as the team with the most championships in NBA history. The Celtics continue to represent Boston's basketball tradition and remain an important part of the city's sports culture.

Research

I am currently developing a new design paradigm for interacting with durable storage that takes into account performance asymmetry between read and write operations, as well as the variable access concurrency that different devices may support. In addition, I am designing new abstractions for data systems architectures supporting efficient hybrid transactional/analytical processing (HTAP). I am also working on designing modern data systems that can support efficient persistent deletes. Prior to joining BU, I worked on medical informatics including software/hardware co-design approaches using various machine learning and embedded system techniques for tele-health monitoring.
