THE CELTICS LEGACY

A franchise unlike any other.

The Boston Celtics are a professional basketball team based in Boston, Massachusetts, and compete in the NBA as a member of the Eastern Conference. Founded in 1946, the Celtics are one of the most historic and successful franchises in basketball, with a legacy built on championship success, legendary players, and a passionate fan base.

Throughout their history, the Celtics have featured some of the greatest players in NBA history, including Bill Russell, Larry Bird, Paul Pierce, and Kevin Garnett. The team dominated the league during the 1950s and 1960s, winning eight consecutive NBA championships from 1959 to 1966. Later generations continued the franchise's tradition of excellence, with championship victories in 1974, 1976, 1981, 1984, 1986, 2008, and 2024.

The Celtics play their home games at TD Garden and are recognized by their iconic green and white colors, shamrock logo, and longstanding rivalry with the Los Angeles Lakers. In 2024, Boston captured its 18th NBA championship, moving ahead of the Lakers for the most championships in NBA history.
