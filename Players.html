THE GREATS WHO BUILT THE LEGACY

Celtics Hall of Famers

These legends helped define the Celtics through championships, individual greatness, and unforgettable moments. Each player below is a member of the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame.

THE DYNASTY

Bill Russell

The centerpiece of Boston's dynasty, Russell won 11 NBA championships as a player and became one of the greatest defenders and leaders in basketball history.

THE 1980s

Larry Bird

A brilliant shooter, passer, and competitor, Bird led Boston to three championships and won three consecutive NBA MVP awards from 1984 to 1986.

ANYTHING IS POSSIBLE

Kevin Garnett

Garnett brought intensity, defense, and leadership to Boston, helping deliver the 2008 championship and earning Defensive Player of the Year in 2008.

This is a selection of Celtics legends, not a complete list of every Hall of Famer who has played for Boston.
